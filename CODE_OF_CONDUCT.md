# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Summary

Be respectful, inclusive, and collaborative. Focus discussions on the
technical substance of CRL / CT / WebPKI tooling. Disagreement is fine;
personal attacks, harassment, and discriminatory language are not.

## Reporting

To report a concern privately, email `support@trustasia.com`. Security
vulnerabilities should follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, edit, remove, or ban contributions or contributors
that violate this code. Decisions are made by simple majority of active
maintainers.

Full text and rationale are available at the link above.
